# Code of Conduct

This project welcomes respectful participation from people of all backgrounds and experience levels. These expectations apply to repository discussions, issues, pull requests and project-related communication.

## Expected conduct

- Be courteous, constructive and patient.
- Critique ideas and code rather than people.
- Respect privacy, boundaries and differing perspectives.
- Acknowledge mistakes and help resolve disagreements productively.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks and disclosure of another person's private information are unacceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

Contact the project maintainer through the [LinkedIn profile listed in the README](https://www.linkedin.com/in/danish-m-khalid/) to report a concern privately. Share only the information necessary to investigate; do not publish private material in an issue.

The maintainer may remove inappropriate content, request corrections or restrict participation in response to violations. Responses should be proportionate to the conduct and its impact. Reports will be handled with care for the privacy of those involved.
